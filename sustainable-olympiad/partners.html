Sponsors & Partners

The Olympiad is free for every student thanks to organisations that share our commitment to education and sustainability.

Our partners

Founding Partner

  • Funds the Olympiad's education programme and student grants.

Gold Partners

  • Provides solar starter kits for Renewable Energy teams.

  • Supports the new Water & Oceans category.

Silver Partners

  • Sponsors the Waste Reduction awards.

  • Offsets travel for finalist teams.

  • Publishes our free classroom resources.

Community Partners

  • Connects teams with local climate groups.

  • Runs prototyping workshops for teams.

  • Advises on accessibility and inclusion.

Become a partner

Partnering with the Sustainable Olympiad connects your organisation with thousands of motivated young innovators, their teachers and their communities.

  • Sponsor a category award or student grant
  • Offer mentoring, workshops or field visits
  • Provide equipment and starter kits
  • Host a regional event or join the jury
