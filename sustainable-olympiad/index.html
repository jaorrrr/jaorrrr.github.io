Why take part?

Teams learn by doing, and leave with skills, friendships and projects that keep making an impact.

  • Solve real problems

    Work on challenges that matter in your own school and community, and measure the difference you make.

  • Learn from experts

    Get feedback from scientists, engineers and entrepreneurs, and connect with students in more than 60 countries.

  • Win grants and recognition

    Medals in every category, certificates for all teams, and grants of up to €5,000 to bring winning ideas to life.

Six challenges, one planet

Every team chooses one category. Each comes with a practical brief for 2026.

  • Renewable Energy

    Design ways to generate, store or save clean energy in your school and community.

  • Waste Reduction & Circular Economy

    Rethink how products are made, used and reused so less ends up in landfill.

  • Sustainable Design & Architecture

    Create products, buildings and public spaces that do more with less.

  • Climate Solutions

    Measure, communicate and act on climate change, locally and globally.

  • Water & Oceans

    Protect fresh water and marine life through smart monitoring and conservation.

  • Biodiversity & Food Systems

    Grow food sustainably and give nature space to thrive.

See all challenges and briefs

How it works

  1. Register your team

    Form a team of 2–5 students with a mentor and choose a challenge.

  2. Round 1: Knowledge Challenge

    Take a 90-minute online quiz on sustainability science from your school.

  3. Round 2: Project Challenge

    Spend ten days building, testing and documenting a real solution.

  4. Global Finals

    Present your project to an international jury in Lisbon, in Brazil or online.
