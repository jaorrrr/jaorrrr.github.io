Schedule & Timeline

Key dates for the 2027 edition, from registration to the Awards Ceremony. All times are Lisbon time (WET/WEST).

Timeline 2027

Status labels update automatically based on today's date.

  1. 1 September 2026 Registration

    Registration opens

    Teams of 2–5 students and a supervising mentor can register online, free of charge.

  2. 15 October 2026 Event

    Information webinar for teachers and mentors

    A one-hour live session on the format, categories and judging. A recording is published afterwards.

  3. 30 November 2026 Deadline

    Early-bird deadline

    Teams registered by this date receive a printed starter kit with sensors and seeds for their project.

  4. 15 January 2027 Deadline

    Registration deadline

    Registration closes at 23:59 Lisbon time. Team details can be edited until Round 1 starts.

  5. 6 February 2027 Competition round

    Round 1: Online Knowledge Challenge

    A 90-minute online quiz on sustainability science, taken by each team from their own school.

  6. 20 February 2027 Results

    Round 1 results

    The top teams in each category and division advance to the Project Challenge.

  7. 1–28 March 2027 Competition round

    Round 2: Project Challenge

    Teams have four weeks to build and test a real solution, then submit a report and a three-minute video.

  8. 9 April 2027 Results

    Finalists announced

    Up to 60 finalist teams are invited to the Global Finals, in person or online.

  9. 22–23 April 2027 Finals

    Global Finals

    Finalists present to an international jury in Lisbon and online, starting on Earth Day.

  10. 24 April 2027 Ceremony

    Awards Ceremony

    Gold, silver and bronze medals in every category, plus the Planet Award for the best overall project. Streamed live.
